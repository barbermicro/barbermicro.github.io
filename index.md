We study bacterial growth and shape, using the tools of modern microscopy, computation, molecular biology and mathematics to tackle fundamental questions in bacterial biology with relevance for human health.
{: .lede}

> **The Barber lab is now open,** and we are always looking for driven, new scientists. If the fundamentals of bacterial growth and form sound like you, get in touch! Please send all inquiries to [barber.527@osu.edu](mailto:barber.527@osu.edu).
